Project 01 · Power BI

Enterprise Power BI & Custom Vega-Lite Reporting Ecosystem

Power BIDenebVega-LiteDAXSVGPBIP

Challenge

Standard visuals could not express what executives actually needed to see: KPI matrices, trends, and exceptions in one glance, kept consistent across many reports and refreshed reliably.

Data Model & SVG / Deneb Architecture

  • Dimensional models in Power BI with version-controlled PBIP enterprise models.
  • Advanced DAX measures and Power Query M for clean, reusable business logic.
  • Deneb / Vega-Lite custom visuals and dynamic SVG matrix KPIs for visuals the out-of-the-box set cannot do.
  • Row-level security so each audience sees only the data meant for them.

Business Impact

Executive-ready reporting that leaders can read in seconds, built on one governed model instead of scattered one-off reports — the same approach behind the Serviceability ROI, Customer QBR, and RUSS dashboards built for Cisco at Zensar.

Project 02 · Automation

Automated Market Intelligence & Intraday Screening Pipeline

Apps ScriptREST APIsAutomated Risk RulesScheduling

Scheduled Data Ingestion

Market data is pulled on a schedule through REST APIs and Google Apps Script pipelines — no manual downloads, no stale spreadsheets.

Catalyst Filtering

  • Screening rules filter the full market down to names with a real catalyst.
  • Automated risk rules flag what does not meet the criteria before it reaches a human.
  • Results are stored in a structured form, ready for review and audit.

Automated Reporting

Screening output is reported automatically on schedule, so the review starts from a short, qualified list instead of a raw market dump.

Architecture
Ingest REST APIs · Scheduled Apps Script ↓ Filter Catalyst screening · Risk rules ↓ Store Structured results · Audit trail ↓ Report Automated scheduled reporting
Project 03 · Telemetry & Fleet

Operational Telemetry & Field Fleet Analytics Platform

Power BIPower AppsPower AutomateTelemetry

Equipment & Battery Lifecycle Tracking

Equipment and battery lifecycle data is tracked end to end — what is in the field, how it is performing, and when it needs attention — instead of living in disconnected logs.

Diagnostic Dashboards

  • Diagnostic dashboards surface faults and trends early, before they become downtime.
  • Clear views by equipment, site, and time, built for field and operations teams.

Power Platform Integration

Power Apps for field input and Power Automate cloud flows for alerts and governance workflows tie the telemetry back into day-to-day operations.
